# Sales kit

Everything for finding and winning the first clients. Fill in `[Company]` once the business has a name.

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `one-pager.md` | The pitch, for the website, a PDF leave-behind, or pasting into an email. |
| `crm-health-report.md` | The entry offer: what it is, what they get, how we quote it, what we need. |
| `outreach.md` | LinkedIn and email messages, referral-partner asks, follow-ups, post ideas. |
| `discovery-call.md` | The script for the first 15–20 conversations, and what to log afterwards. |
| `targets.md` | Who to contact, where to find them, and the weekly routine for 20 hours a week. |

**The rule for confidentiality:** the first company can be named in conversation, but never use its figures, customers or details beyond "a multi-branch commercial contractor".
