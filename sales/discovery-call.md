# Discovery call (20–30 minutes)

**The goal:** understand their pain well enough to know whether you can help, and agree one next step. Listen for 70% of the call. Don't demo until you know what to show.

## 1. Open (2 min)
> "Thanks for the time. I'd like to understand how you run the business on numbers today and where it hurts. If there's a fit, I'll tell you what I'd suggest; if not, I'll say so. Sound OK?"

## 2. How it works today (8 min)
- "Walk me through how leadership gets its numbers each week or month. Who builds them, and how long does it take?"
- "Which systems hold the data? (CRM, accounting, projects, HR, spreadsheets.) Who owns each?"
- "Which numbers does leadership argue about most?"
- "When was the last time a number turned out to be wrong? What happened?"

## 3. The pain and its cost (8 min)
- "If you could fix one thing about your reporting tomorrow, what would it be?"
- "What does that cost you today: hours, bad decisions, missed deals, late cash?"
- "What have you tried? (A BI tool, an analyst, the CRM's own reports, a consultant.) Why didn't it stick?"
- "Is anyone looking at AI tools? What do you want them to do?"

## 4. Show, only what's relevant (5 min, optional)
Open the Bluestem demo on whichever page matches their pain:

| Their pain | Show them |
|---|---|
| Report building | **Scorecard** |
| Trust in the numbers | **Clean-up list** |
| Pipeline | **Pipeline**, including the past-due bar |
| Cash | **Receivables** |
| AI | **Ask AI** |

> "This is a fictional company, but it's the same system I'd set up for you, on your data."

## 5. Next step (3 min)
- **Good fit:** "The easiest way to see what's really in your data is the CRM Health Report: one week, read-only, fixed price, and you don't pay if it doesn't find something worth fixing. Want me to send it over?"
- **Not yet:** "Sounds like the timing isn't right. Could I check back in [month]?" Set a next-step date in the hub.
- **Not a fit:** say so, and ask, "Who do you know who has this problem?"

## Red flags (be polite, move on)
- No CRM or accounting system with real data in it.
- "We just need a dashboard by Friday."
- Nobody in leadership owns the numbers.
- They want us to replace or implement their CRM or ERP.

## Right after the call (5 min, in the company hub)
1. Log the conversation: their words for the pain, their systems, who decides, any budget or timing.
2. Set the stage (Conversation, or Proposal if they asked for the Health Report) and the estimated value.
3. Set the next step and its date.
4. Send the follow-up email the same day.
