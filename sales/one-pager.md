**For COOs, CFOs and owners of small and mid-size companies, in any industry.**

## Sound familiar?
- Your data lives in ten places: the CRM, accounting, project tools and a pile of spreadsheets.
- Someone spends every Monday copying numbers between systems to build the leadership report.
- Sales and finance bring different numbers to the same meeting.
- You bought Copilot or ChatGPT, and it can't tell you a single thing about your own business.

## What we do
We connect the systems your company already runs on into one place. Then we build what that makes possible:
- reports and a weekly scorecard that build themselves, with every number clicking through to the records behind it;
- tools that replace the spreadsheets your team rebuilds every week;
- alerts when something important moves, like a big deal slipping or a customer going 90 days late;
- AI that answers questions about *your* business from your own data;
- **AI agents that do the work:** daily briefs, drafted reports, meeting prep and follow-ups. A person approves anything that goes out.

## How it works
1. **A conversation.** How your company runs, which systems hold your data, and what you wish you could see and do.
2. **A custom proposal.** A written plan and fixed quote built around your systems, your data and what matters most to you.
3. **We build it.** We bring your data together and reproduce your existing reports *to the cent*, so you can trust them. Then the tools, the AI and the agents.
4. **Run & Grow (monthly).** We keep it accurate, add what you need next, and review it with your leadership every month.
