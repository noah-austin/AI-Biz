# CRM Health Report

**One week. Fixed price. Read-only. You'll see exactly what your CRM is hiding, and how to fix it.**

## Who it's for
Companies whose leadership numbers come from a CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics, Pipedrive) and who suspect the numbers aren't quite right.

## What you get
1. **A written report** (and a 45-minute walkthrough) showing:
   - **Wins your reports can't see:** won deals with placeholder dates, missing amounts or the wrong stage.
   - **A pipeline that isn't real:** open deals whose close date passed months ago, and duplicates that count twice.
   - **Gaps that break your metrics:** missing margins, missing loss reasons, deals with no owner or branch.
   - **What it means in dollars:** how much pipeline is inflated and how many wins are under-counted.
2. **The fix list:** every record to fix, with a direct link, grouped by owner, so your team can clean it up in an afternoon.
3. **Three opportunities:** the reports and tools that would make the biggest difference for you, with a rough plan and price.

## How it works
| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | 30-minute kickoff. You create a read-only CRM login for us (we'll send the steps). |
| 2–4 | We connect, run our checks and reproduce one report you already use, to check it against yours. |
| 5 | Walkthrough and written report. |

## What we need from you
- A read-only CRM user (we never change anything in your systems).
- One report your leadership already uses, so we can show where the numbers differ and why.
- 30 minutes at kickoff and 45 at the walkthrough.

## Price
Priced to their setup: how many systems we connect and how many records and fields we check. They get a fixed quote after the first call, before any work starts. Don't publish a price. (Internal guide only: most land around $1,500–2,500; founding clients get a discount.)
- **Guarantee:** if the report doesn't show you something worth fixing, you don't pay.
- **Credit:** if you go on to an Assessment, the full fee counts toward it.

## Your data
Read-only access only, deleted at the end unless you continue with us. Covered by a short confidentiality agreement signed before kickoff. Nothing leaves our secure server, and nothing is shared or reused.
