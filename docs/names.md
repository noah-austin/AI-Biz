# Business name shortlist

*2026-10-17. Style: plain and credible. Each name works on its own or with "Data Co." / "Data" after it.*

## How these were checked
- **Same-name screen (done):** web searches for companies with the same or a confusingly similar name in data, software, consulting or field service.
- **.com availability (still to do, by Noah):** this sandbox can't reach domain registries. Check each address below at a registrar (Google/Squarespace Domains, Cloudflare, Namecheap). About 2 minutes.
- **Before committing:** Texas SOSDirect name search, a USPTO trademark search (classes 35 and 42), and the LinkedIn company name.

## The shortlist

| Name | Why it fits | Same-name screen | .com to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Joinery** (Joinery Data Co.) | Joinery is the craft of fitting separate pieces together so they hold as one. That's the business: we join the systems you already have, then build on them. Short, memorable, a little craft-like. | Clean in data and consulting. Only woodworking-software firms use it, mostly in the UK. | joinery.co · joinerydata.com · joinerydataco.com · thejoinery.co |
| **Common Source** (Common Source Data) | "One common source" everyone works from: the plainest way to say "single source of truth" without the jargon. Sounds like an established firm. | Clean. Slight echo of "open source". | commonsource.com · commonsourcedata.com · commonsource.co |
| **Onestead** | Invented: "one" + "stead" (place), so it literally means "one place". Unique and easy to own. | Clean, but being invented makes it less "plain". | onestead.com · onesteaddata.com |
| **Tributary** (Tributary Data) | Many streams feeding one river, like many systems feeding one place. | Small conflict: a two-person "Tributary Group" software holding company in Austin. | tributarydata.com · tributary.co |
| **Mainspring** | The spring that powers a watch: the foundation everything runs on. | Busy name: Mainspring Energy (large) and two smaller software/consulting firms. | mainspringdata.com |

## Dropped after screening
- **Plainview Data:** too close to *Planview*, an Austin enterprise-software company.
- **Keel:** Keel Infrastructure (Nasdaq: KEEL, data centers) and a Keel & Company NetSuite/Dynamics consultancy.
- **Truss:** a Truss software consultancy and a Truss real-estate-data company.
- **Fieldstone:** a Fieldstone field-service software product for HVAC shops, which is part of our market.
- **Cairn:** "CairnMetrics", a 2026 dashboard product for small businesses that unifies their data. Too close.
- **Throughline:** Throughline, Inc., a 99-person consultancy, plus a product-analytics startup.
- **Wellspring, Headwater:** existing software and data-science companies.
- **Groundwork, Bedrock, Cornerstone, Keystone, Clearline:** crowded names.

## Recommendation
**Joinery.** It tells the story in one word (we join what you have and build on it). It stands out among "-data" and "-analytics" names, and the search came back clean in our field. **Common Source** is the safer, more corporate second choice.
