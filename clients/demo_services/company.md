# Kestrel Ridge Engineering (fictional demo company)

**Everything here is made up.** Kestrel Ridge is the professional-services firm we show prospects in engineering,
consulting, architecture, IT services and other firms that sell time.

## The story
- Civil, structural and building-systems engineering for municipalities, school districts, developers, health systems,
  industrial clients and state agencies, from four Texas offices (Houston, Dallas, Austin, San Antonio).
- About 100 people and $17M a year. Work is sold as fixed-fee design projects, hourly studies and on-call retainers.
- Systems: CRM (proposals), a project and time system (projects, budgets, weekly time by person and project),
  accounting (monthly invoices per project) and HR/payroll.

## Stories in the data
- **Austin lost its biggest client in February:** same people, a fifth less work, utilization down from about 74% to 58%.
- **Houston is stretched:** the highest utilization (about 85%) and overtime for its hourly designers and inspectors.
- **One Dallas project manager's fixed fees run about a third over budget:** realization around 72% against 95% firm-wide.
- **State agencies push back on every invoice:** realization about 87%.
- **A large hourly study in Houston hasn't been billed since July.**

## Problems planted in the data (what Data checks finds)
Hourly time not billed for 60+ days, fixed-fee projects past their budget hours, two engineers on a lapsed PE license,
staff with no manager, a missing timesheet, plus the CRM problems every demo has.

## A 10-minute demo script
1. **Projects → utilization by office.** "Austin has room for a fifth more work. Houston is running hot." Point at the
   12-month trend: the drop in February.
2. **Realization by project manager.** One PM's fixed fees run a third over: "you'd find this at year-end; here it's weekly."
3. **Projects burning ahead of progress.** Budget used against percent complete: which jobs need a change order now.
4. **Unbilled hourly work.** $41K of work nobody invoiced since July.
5. **People, Scorecard, AI agents, Data checks** as in the Bluestem script.
