# Cottonwood Precision Manufacturing (fictional demo company)

**Everything here is made up.** Cottonwood is the manufacturer we show prospects in manufacturing and fabrication.

## The story
- Contract fabricator and machine shop: brackets and weldments, machined parts, electrical enclosures, trailer components
  and custom fabrication for equipment makers, from plants in Fort Worth and Waco. About $37M a year and 110 people.
- Customer orders (EDI, email and blanket releases) ship from finished goods; production orders run through seven work
  centers; raw steel, aluminum, coatings and consumables are bought weekly.
- Systems: CRM (quotes and blanket orders), ERP orders and shipping, a shop-floor system (production orders, downtime),
  inventory (raw materials and finished goods), purchasing, accounting and HR/payroll.

## Stories in the data
- **The old Waco press brake keeps breaking down:** about 45% effective and 49 hours down in a month, so production there
  runs late and Waco customers get about 79% of order lines complete.
- **CNC scrap jumped to about 7% in July** after two experienced Fort Worth machinists left (the shop runs about 2%).
- **Steel prices are up about 12% a year** and the steel service center delivers late; customer prices were set in January,
  so margin on steel-heavy trailer components is the thinnest.
- **Two raw materials are out of stock.**

## Problems planted in the data (what Data checks finds)
Late production orders, orders with more than 10% scrap, orders shipped but not invoiced, negative stock, an item with no
cost, received orders with no vendor bill, the steel vendor set up twice, two welders on a lapsed certification, staff
with no manager, missing timesheets, plus the CRM problems.

## A 10-minute demo script
1. **Production.** "Every machine's effectiveness, downtime by reason and scrap, every morning." Point at the Waco press
   brake and its breakdowns, then at the CNC scrap trend.
2. **Orders.** The same problem as customers feel it: Waco's fill rate and the backorders.
3. **Margin by product line.** Steel costs rising under January prices.
4. **Inventory, Purchasing, People.** The stock-outs, the late steel vendor, the machinists who left.
5. **Scorecard, AI agents, Data checks** as in the Bluestem script.
