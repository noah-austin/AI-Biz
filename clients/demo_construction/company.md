# Mesquite Ridge Constructors (fictional demo company)

**Everything here is made up.** Mesquite Ridge is the general contractor we show prospects in construction and the trades.

## The story
- Commercial general contractor: healthcare, K-12 schools, office and tenant finish, industrial and warehouse, retail and
  public works, from three Texas offices (Houston, San Antonio, Austin). About $90M a year and 120 people on payroll.
- Work is won by hard bid and negotiated contracts, plus small service work. Owners hold back 10% retainage (5% on service work).
- Systems: CRM (bids), job cost (jobs, change orders, monthly costs, pay applications), accounting (an invoice per pay
  application, Reference = the CRM deal), purchasing (subcontracts, materials, rentals) and HR/payroll.

## Stories in the data
- **Margin is fading:** active jobs are projected about 2 points below what they were bid at, most of it on one Houston
  project manager's jobs.
- **Two jobs are projected to lose money:** a big San Antonio job that ran over, and one of the fading Houston jobs.
- **One Austin job bills a month behind its progress:** under-billed by about $300K.
- **Six change orders worth about $345K have waited on owners for months.**
- **San Antonio is short of field crew:** overtime runs about 15%.

## Problems planted in the data (what Data checks finds)
A job losing money, an under-billed job, old unsigned change orders, received purchase orders with no vendor bill, a
duplicate vendor, two lapsed crane operator certifications, staff with no manager, missing timesheets, plus the CRM problems.

## A 10-minute demo script
1. **Job cost → work in progress.** "Your month-end WIP schedule, every morning instead of three weeks after month-end."
2. **Margin by project manager.** Where the fade is, by person, in dollars.
3. **Over and under billing.** The job you're financing for the owner.
4. **Change orders waiting.** Work done and not yet agreed.
5. **People, Purchasing, Scorecard, AI agents, Data checks** as in the Bluestem script.
