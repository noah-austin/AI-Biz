# Bramblewood Industrial Supply (fictional demo company)

**Everything here is made up.** Bramblewood is the distributor we show prospects in distribution and wholesale.

## The story
- Industrial and MRO supply distributor: pipe, valves and fittings, fasteners, safety gear, electrical, tools, janitorial
  and HVAC/R supplies, sold to mechanical contractors, plants, facilities, oilfield services and municipalities.
- Six Texas branches in three regions (Gulf Coast: Houston, Beaumont; South Texas: San Antonio, Corpus Christi;
  North & Central: Dallas, Austin). About $40M a year in sales at around 25% gross margin; about 85 people.
- Systems: CRM (quotes, project bids and supply agreements), an ERP for orders and shipping, accounting (an invoice per
  shipped order), inventory (one warehouse per branch), purchasing and HR/payroll.

## Stories in the data
- **Corpus Christi is two warehouse staff short:** the highest overtime, only about 79% of order lines shipped complete
  first time (others 92–96%), and two of its fastest-moving items out of stock.
- **Safety & PPE margin slid from 23% to 17% in a year:** the vendor raised costs about 10% and prices didn't follow.
- **One Dallas rep discounts heavily:** about 16% margin against about 25% for everyone else.
- **The valve vendor delivers late** about a third of the time, so valves backorder more.
- **The biggest Houston customer started paying slowly** in spring.

## Problems planted in the data (what Data checks finds)
Orders shipped but never invoiced, lines priced below cost, negative stock, an item with no cost, received purchase orders
with no vendor bill, the same vendor set up twice, staff with no manager, a missing timesheet, two drivers on a lapsed
commercial driver's license, plus the CRM problems every demo has (placeholder close dates, duplicates, stale deals,
missing margins, lost deals with no reason, unlinked accounting customers).

## A 10-minute demo script
1. **Orders.** "Fill rate, shipping on time and margin, by branch, product line and rep, every morning." Point at Corpus
   Christi, then at the margin slipping on Safety & PPE: "nobody noticed the vendor's price increases weren't passed on."
2. **Margin by rep.** One rep is giving away about 9 points of margin.
3. **Inventory → Reorder now.** The two Corpus Christi stock-outs are at the top; dead stock below.
4. **People.** Corpus Christi's overtime and open roles: the same story from payroll.
5. **Scorecard, AI agents, Data checks** as in the Bluestem script.
